# Unit Economics — AI Spend Audit

## Lead Value
Credex operates as an AI credit marketplace. For every user that finds >$500/month in savings, the potential value of that lead to Credex is high. Switching a startup to discounted infrastructure can yield commissions or service fees.

## Conversion Math (Projections)
- **Traffic Source:** Organic developer communities (Reddit, X).
- **Target Conversion:** 3% of auditors booking a Credex consultation.
- **Estimated CAC:** $0 (Organic growth).
- **LTV (Lifetime Value):** $1,200+ per successfully switched company.

## Profitability
By automating the audit process, Credex reduces the cost of "Sales Discovery." Instead of a human salesperson analyzing a bill, this tool does it in 60 seconds, allowing the team to focus only on high-value leads.

# Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

## Target Audience
- **Primary:** Engineering Managers (EMs) and Founders at seed-to-Series A startups.
- **Pain Point:** Hidden "SaaS sprawl" where they pay for 10 seats of a tool but only use 3.

## Distribution Channels
1. **Developer Subreddits:** r/startups and r/webdev (sharing as a "free tool for the community").
2. **X (Twitter):** Targeting the #buildinpublic community where founders share their "burn rates."
3. **Hacker News:** Launching as a "Show HN" project.

## The Viral Loop
Each audit generates a unique public URL. When a founder sees they can save $2,000/year, they are incentivized to share that screenshot/link on LinkedIn or X to show they are "optimizing operations," which brings more users to the tool.
